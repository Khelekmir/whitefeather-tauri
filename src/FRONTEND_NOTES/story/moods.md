## ⚙️ **Game-Design-Oriented Mood Ideas**

(Useful if you’re planning modifiers for gameplay mechanics)

* **Focused** → accuracy or perception boost
* **Agitated** → damage output ↑, defense ↓
* **Euphoric** → charisma or charm boost
* **Hopeless** → motivation drain
* **Curious** → exploration or discovery buff
* **Paranoid** → evasion boost, trust ↓
* **Stoic** → emotional resistance, stable defense
* **Inspired** → creative or magical power ↑
* **Vengeful** → attack ↑ after being harmed
* **Distracted** → accuracy ↓, awareness ↓
* **Fearless** → immune to panic effects
* **Exhausted** → stamina ↓, defense ↓
* **Focused Rage** → attack ↑, control ↓
