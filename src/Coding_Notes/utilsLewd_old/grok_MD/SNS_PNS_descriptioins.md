For PNS (parasympathetic nervous system), activations are typically increases in relaxation or arousal contexts (e.g., up to ~5-6x in our sexual model, with a climax threshold at 5x). In stress scenarios, PNS suppresses below 1x (clamped at 0.2x min), so I've noted equivalent suppression levels for distinction. Presentations are qualitative summaries; higher PNS promotes "rest-and-digest" or genital arousal, but excessive can lead to bradycardia or over-relaxation.
