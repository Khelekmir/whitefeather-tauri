Absolutely! Here's a concise list of common historical shield types arranged **from smallest to largest**, along with a description of their general **size and purpose**—ideal for use in a fantasy RPG:

---

### 🛡️ **Shields by Size (Smallest to Largest)**

1. **Buckler**

   * **Size**: \~6–18 inches (15–45 cm) diameter
   * **Description**: A small, handheld shield usually made of metal, held in the fist. Used primarily for parrying rather than full-body defense. Perfect for rogues, duelists, or swashbucklers.

2. **Targe**

   * **Size**: \~18–24 inches (45–60 cm) diameter
   * **Description**: A small round shield, often strapped to the arm. Common in Scottish Highland warfare. Offers more coverage than a buckler, while still allowing mobility.

3. **Heater Shield**

   * **Size**: \~20–30 inches (50–75 cm) tall
   * **Description**: A classic medieval knight shield with a distinct triangular shape. Balanced between protection and agility, often emblazoned with heraldry.

4. **Kite Shield**

   * **Size**: \~36–48 inches (90–120 cm) tall
   * **Description**: Long and tapering, excellent for mounted combat and protecting the body from neck to shin. Often seen in Norman and Crusader contexts.

5. **Round Shield (e.g., Viking)**

   * **Size**: \~30–36 inches (75–90 cm) diameter
   * **Description**: A broad, circular shield often made of wood with a metal boss in the center. Used for both defense and offense (e.g., shield wall formations). Size can vary, but generally larger than targes.

6. **Tower Shield (Pavise)**

   * **Size**: \~48–60 inches (120–150 cm) tall
   * **Description**: A massive shield that covers nearly the whole body. Often used by crossbowmen or heavy infantry. Provides excellent cover but is very cumbersome.

---

### Optional Additions:

* **Scutum (Roman Shield)**: \~42 inches (105 cm) tall, curved rectangular shape—somewhere between kite and tower size.
* **Aspis (Greek Hoplite Shield)**: \~36 inches (90 cm) diameter, thick and heavy; could slot between round and kite shields in function.
