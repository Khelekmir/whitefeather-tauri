### **Days 1 to 5:** 
- At the beginning of the cycle, discharge is usually red or bloody as the body sheds the uterine lining.
### **Days 6 to 12:**
- Following a period, a person may notice less vaginal discharge than usual. As the egg starts to develop and mature, the cervical mucus will become cloudy and white or yellow. It may feel sticky.
### **Days 13 to 15:**
- During the ovulation phase, the mucus will be thin and slippery, similar to the consistency of egg whites. After ovulation, the mucus will return to cloudy, white, or yellow, and possibly sticky or tacky.
### **Days 16 to 28:**
- The cervical mucus will lighten, and a person will see less of it, before getting another period.
